## 1 Data completeness
The six fields are current valid-record percentages, not ability measures. Overall = .50 decisions + .15 timing + .15 evidence-reference integrity + .10 estimates + .10 reasons. Evidence completeness can be 100 with no evidence opened.

## 2 OSI overall and phase
Option orientation is (strategicLoading-operationalLoading)/(sum+1e-9), centered and maximum-absolute-normalized within the actually available action set. A2 is excluded from OSI. Phase means are A/B/C/D; overall requires all four.

## 3 Operational/Strategic Strength
Absolute operationalLoading and strategicLoading are phase-balanced and ×100, separate from centered OSI.

## 4 Integration/Dispersion
Integration is 2*O*S/(O+S), null for missing/zero denominator. Dispersion is the population SD of available phase OSI, null below two phases. They are not a general decision-quality score.

## 5 Canonical cognitive constructs
The eleven keys in the field table are the exact current construct set. Information Discipline is ALWAYS null/not measured. Risk Posture is -1..1, relative and non-normative. MultiDomainIntegration uses existing nine-domain salience entropy; it is not the orientation harmonic-integration field.

## 6 Decision timing/revisions
Runtime raw input: decisions[].enteredAtMs/confirmedAtMs/elapsedMs/activeDecisionMs/hiddenTabMs/revisionCount/selectionHistory. Current result exposes median/IQR active milliseconds, total revisions, revised windows and evidence-triggered revision rate. R4/R6/R8 reason time is outside the confirmed decision timing. Repeated same-action clicks do not add a revision. Synthetic calibration driver timestamps must not be interpreted as human timing.

## 7 Evidence-use behavior
Runtime evidence[], evidenceOpens[].windowId/openedAtMs/closedAtMs/activeDwellMs, decisions[].availableEvidenceIds/openedEvidenceIds are available post-run. Explicit detail opens only count. InformationSeeking combines existing option loading plus relevant evidence coverage/diversity, not mission correctness. The aggregate result does not expose total evidence dwell or opened-source entropy separately. Current AAR shows per-item opened/not-opened.

## 8 Belief/updating behavior
Four runtime beliefs have checkpointId, recordedAtMs, mostLikelyCorridorId, corridorConfidence, targetedInterferenceEstimate, targetedInterferenceConfidence, visibleEvidenceIdsAtCheckpoint. All are available for later context-specific plots; the current cognitive result exposes evidenceResponsiveUpdating, based only on targeted-estimate changes versus visible-evidence support changes. Corridor correctness belongs to domain output, not cognitive correctness points. Reasons expose phaseId/reasonId/recordedAtMs/capturedBeforeOutcome; current decision coherence uses the existing frozen reason-alignment/cross-decision matrices.

## 9 Mission/domain outputs must remain separate
missionEndState; domain.gnssPntAssessment/navigationIntegrity/criticalDelivery/logisticsResilience/delayControl/civilianImpactProxy/networkLogisticsBalance are descriptive mission/domain outputs. Neither outcome nor winning generates cognitive points. Domain checkpoints may compare beliefs to truth only after completion. Domain exposure and Actor delay decomposition must not be relabelled cognitive ability.

## 10 Hidden/audit-only fields
worldId, seed, true primary corridor, Actor profile/objective/internal posture, hidden provenance and Actor delay decomposition are research/AAR context, not live player evidence. Metadata version/validity fields are audit context, not a score.

## 11 No global Cognitive Score
No sum/average of OSI, integration, constructs, completeness or mission success is defined. Risk Posture is non-normative. Unmeasured Information Discipline cannot be assigned zero or inferred from other measures. No human, psychometric, intelligence, personality or cross-person validity is claimed.

## 12 Candidate cross-scenario constructs and data requirements
Active product Scenario 1 is internal Scenario 4 (App released mapping id4), not legacy id1. Its Cognitive V3 input requires records, telemetry, attribution estimates, move reasons and resourceEvents; outputs have three per-move orientation scores, response-process flags, per-window audits and timing dwell in addition to shared-looking constructs. S5 requires eight decisions, four corridor/targeted beliefs, B/C/D reasons, visible-evidence/open telemetry and resource snapshots. Scenario-4 has coalitionOrientation and a measured informationDiscipline path; S5 has no coalition construct and informationDiscipline is null. Similar names are candidates only: available-option normalization, task windows, evidence schemas, phase weights, expert versions and null rules differ. No cross-scenario dashboard, harmonization formula or Scenario-1 change is made.
