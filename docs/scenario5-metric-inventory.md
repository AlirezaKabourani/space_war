# Scenario 5 — Exhaustive Metric / Indicator Inventory

## 1. Executive summary

This inventory covers the current accepted Scenario 5 after Phase 08B. It contains **314** indicators: 223 single-run-capable, 100 multi-run-capable, 110 cognitive/behavioral/evidence/timing indicators, and 75 mission/domain indicators. It introduces no formula into the application. Every row is tied to current source or is explicitly marked `not_measured`.

The cognitive outputs are context-dependent behavioral proxies. They are not intelligence, personality, stable ability, psychometric validity, or one global cognitive score. Synthetic calibration frequencies are not real-world probabilities.

## 17. Scientific limitations

- Cognitive loadings, reason weights, coherence matrices, recognition thresholds, mission thresholds, and calibration warning thresholds are internal engineering definitions pending expert/human validation.
- Hidden truth is used only for post-completion accuracy/audit fields and is firewalled from the Cognitive V3 telemetry bundle and player view.
- Scenario 5 has no global cognitive score and currently does not measure Information Discipline.
- Mission and domain outputs describe the synthetic mechanics; they are not empirical estimates of operational probabilities or real civilian harm.
- Cross-scenario candidates require semantic alignment and adapter review before aggregation; same names alone are insufficient.
